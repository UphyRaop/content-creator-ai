# CCAI Roadmap and Progress

## Product definition
CCAI is an AI-powered video creation application. Social account management, publishing automation, and social analytics are out of scope.

## Approved 13-stage production workflow
1. Idea and reference input
2. Creative brief approval
3. AI script generation
4. Script editing and approval
5. Scene breakdown
6. AI image generation
7. Individual scene approval and regeneration
8. AI voice generation
9. Video timeline and editing
10. Video rendering
11. Post-production
12. Thumbnail generation
13. Video export

Approval gates are mandatory: no script before brief approval; no scene breakdown before script approval; no voice before every scene is approved. Editing approved brief/script content clears its approval and dependent downstream work. Simulated responses are always identified as simulations.

## Phase 9 verification record
- The post-production studio accepts uploaded background music, voice recordings and effects; displays browser-decoded local audio waveforms with trim-window markers (and an accessible fallback); supports enable, volume, source trim, fade-in/out and positive synchronization delay. Music and automatic ducking default off. Edits and revisions are non-destructive and retain the original render ID/version.
- The validated post-production model creates immutable render-linked revisions and derived timing plans. Its Node-only endpoint accepts a completed render job ID and exact source version, never a caller-supplied filesystem path. JSON requests cap at 32 MiB; up to 12 tracks, 8 MiB per upload and 24 MiB total. Audio types, durations, trim/fade/volume/duck settings and duck targets are checked; temporary asset names are generated server-side.
- The FFmpeg queue mixes the original audio with uploaded tracks, applies trims, delay, volume, fades and opt-in sidechain ducking, and ffprobe-validates a new MP4. The original file is byte-for-byte preserved. Derived jobs share status, progress, cancel, retry, stream and download routes. Render history and temporary files are process-local for one hour.
- Node mode submits and polls real derived jobs when a completed source render/version exists. GitHub Pages stays local-storage simulation only, never calls the post-production API, and clearly states no MP4 is created.
- Automated evidence on 19 November 2026 (Malaysia time): `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/test-post-production.mjs` and `scripts/test-post-production-render.mjs` passed; the latter confirmed a derived MP4 with video/audio streams, 1920x1080 dimensions, expected duration, and unchanged parent bytes. `scripts/test-ffmpeg-renderer.mjs`, `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npm run build:static`, `npm run build`, inline JS `node --check`, and `git diff --check` passed.
